The Framework

Identify → Quantify → Rectify

Three words are the spine of the entire product. Each is a discipline: reveal the actor, price the damage, and build the pathway that turns truth into consequence.

The three stages

01 / IDENTIFY

Reveal who is transgressing, and what activity creates the harm.

Not "pollution exists," but "this actor, through this activity, in this location, is creating this specific boundary pressure."

Site → Operator → Parent Company → Activity → Boundary Pressure → Affected Communities & Ecosystems

02 / QUANTIFY

Turn harm into numbers.

Estimate how much damage, how severe, how long it lasts, what it costs to restore, and what it costs if ignored — converted into Planetary Debt.

Severity × Duration × Restoration Cost → Planetary Debt

03 / RECTIFY

The action layer.

Verity doesn't fine companies — only governments, courts, contracts, insurers, lenders, and markets can. Verity builds the truth infrastructure that lets those consequences happen.

Truth → Debt → Action
