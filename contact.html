About ReVerity

About ReVerity Ledger

ReVerity Ledger is building the accounting and software infrastructure required to make environmental harm financially accountable. Its mission is to help government identify environmental debt, quantify public burden, support lawful collection mechanisms, protect restoration funding, and verify measurable environmental outcomes.

Mission

Make environmental damage financially accountable.

Vision

A future in which economic profit can no longer be separated from the environmental cost required to produce it.

The Operating Command

Identify. Quantify. Rectify.
