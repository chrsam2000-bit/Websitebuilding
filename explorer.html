When environmental harm never enters the operator’s cost structure, the expense does not disappear — it transfers. Federal budgets absorb remediation, states absorb infrastructure strain, municipalities absorb water treatment, ratepayers absorb pass-through costs, public-health systems absorb exposure outcomes, and future generations inherit the unresolved balance. Public burden accounting makes that transfer visible, sector by sector.

Dispute & Correction Process

A ledger is only as strong as its right of correction.

This platform never rests on unsupported accusations. Every record carries a formal path to challenge it: disputes are logged, versioned, and publicly traceable, and every correction becomes part of the permanent audit history of the record it amends.

DC-01

Submit a Correction

File a structured correction against any figure, attribution, or classification in an entity record. Each submission receives a tracked reference.

Open a correction
DC-02

Upload Supporting Evidence

Attach monitoring data, filings, laboratory results, or remediation documentation to strengthen or contest an existing record.

Submit evidence
DC-03

Contest a Methodology

Challenge the calculation method behind a modeled liability. Methodology disputes are reviewed against the published standards library.

Review methodologies
DC-04

Flag Outdated Information

Identify records superseded by newer filings, completed remediation, ownership changes, or corrected agency data.

Flag a record
DC-05

Request Formal Review

Escalate a contested record to structured review with documented findings, response timelines, and a published disposition.

Request review
DC-06

View Version History

Every record is version-controlled. See what changed, when, on what evidence, and at whose request — nothing is silently rewritten.

See how versioning works
