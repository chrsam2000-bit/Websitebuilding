The Broken Equation

Private actors can capture the profit while the public inherits the damage.

Profit is captured privately. Damage is absorbed publicly. Restoration remains underfunded. ReVerity Ledger exists to place harmful economic actors directly inside the financial cost structure.

Ledger Entry 01

Private Profit

Economic benefit captured by the operator — booked, announced, and rewarded, with none of the environmental cost on the balance sheet.

Ledger Entry 02

Public Burden

Cleanup, health, infrastructure, disaster, and future-government costs transferred to taxpayers, ratepayers, and communities.

Ledger Entry 03

Restoration Gap

The difference between the damage created and the money actually available to repair it — the unpaid national environmental debt.

The ReVerity System

Six disciplines. One unbroken chain.

From the first record of harm to the last verified restoration outcome, every step is part of a single accountable system.

01 / IDENTIFY

Identify

Map actors, facilities, activities, environmental harms, locations, and affected communities into a single national registry.

02 / QUANTIFY

Quantify

Convert evidence into environmental debt estimates using category-specific, confidence-graded methodologies.

03 / RECTIFY

Rectify

Support lawful federal accountability, assessment, compliance, and dispute processes — with correction rights built in.

04 / FUND

Fund

Connect collected payments to a legally protected governmental funding structure with full source-to-use traceability.

05 / DEPLOY

Deploy

Direct funding toward qualified restoration and market-transition projects through transparent, competitive procurement.

06 / RESTORE

Restore

Measure whether funded projects reduce environmental debt and improve real-world conditions — verified, not assumed.

The Fund Architecture

From environmental harm to protected restoration capital.

ReVerity Ledger is designed to support a lawful governmental system in which environmental assessments, fees, settlements, taxes, penalties, and restoration obligations can be routed into a dedicated public fund for environmental restoration, public-health protection, future-resource protection, and transition away from harmful systems.

Proposed Fund

Primary Restoration
and Transition Trust

A proposed legally protected governmental pool dedicated to environmental restoration and market transition.

Collection alone does not create restoration. Protection of the fund does.

Governmental control · Statutory protection · Audited allocation

ReVerity does not independently levy taxes or hold government funds. Government authorities authorize, collect, control, and allocate public money. ReVerity provides accounting, assessment, routing, transparency, and verification infrastructure.

Assessment Authorized by Government

Legal authority establishes the environmental debt assessment for measured harm.

Government Collection

Public authorities collect payments through lawful mechanisms — taxes, fees, settlements, penalties, obligations.

Primary Restoration and Transition Trust

Collected funds enter a dedicated, protected governmental pool rather than dissolving into general revenue.

Restoration and Transition Allocation

Funding is directed to qualified projects through transparent scoring, procurement, and anti-fraud controls.

Verified Environmental Outcomes

Every allocation is traced to measured, independently verified reduction of environmental debt.
