The Platform

Infrastructure modules for the environmental ledger.

ReVerity Ledger is not a suite of consulting packages. It is one federal-grade environmental debt accounting system, delivered as eight interoperable infrastructure modules — a single system of record for public burden, restoration funding, and market transition, serving government, regulated organizations, counsel, and restoration developers.

Module 01

Federal Environmental Debt Ledger

For Federal agencies · Treasury · OMB · EPA · Congressional offices · Federal auditors

Module 01 — National System of Record

The national balance sheet for environmental debt.

The core module of the platform: a national liability registry that records environmental harm as financial liability, maps it to facilities and parent companies, and states it in annual environmental debt statements built to a federal audit standard. It is the reference against which every other module reconciles.

A liability that never appears on a ledger is a liability the public pays twice.

Module 03

Corporate Environmental Debt Filing

For Covered companies and facilities · Filing officers · Corporate counsel

Module 03 — Annual Filing Infrastructure

Built to the standard of a tax filing, not a sustainability report.

This is not optional ESG reporting. Corporate Environmental Debt Filing is prototype infrastructure for future mandatory or government-authorized environmental debt filing systems — a full filing workflow with evidence, calculation, dispute rights, and a closing statement, designed so environmental financial accountability can be administered the way tax obligations already are.

Filers receive what any legitimate filing system owes them: clear methodology, correction rights, and a defensible record.

Module 04

Environmental Tax and Liability Advisory

For Covered companies · General counsel · Finance and transaction teams

A senior advisory practice operated to the standard of an elite tax engagement. Module 05

Legacy Environmental Debt Register

For Governments · Redevelopment authorities · Federal legacy-site programs

Much of the national environmental debt has no active operator attached to it — only a site, a community, and an unresolved record. The Legacy Register gives that debt a permanent accounting entry: it maps the site, traces the historical responsible parties where records allow, and states the potential public burden until the obligation is resolved.

Module 06

Primary Restoration and Transition Trust — Administration Layer

For Government trust administrators · Treasury and comptroller functions · Independent auditors

Module 06 — Fund Records & Controls

The controls layer for protected restoration funding.

Collection alone does not create restoration — protection of the fund does. The Administration Layer provides the record-keeping, controls, and verification infrastructure for a governmental restoration trust: every dollar traceable from source to use, every allocation scored and audited, every environmental outcome verified rather than assumed.

Actual funds remain under governmental control at all times. ReVerity administers records, controls, and verification — never the money itself.

Module 07

Restoration Project Marketplace

For Government procurement offices · Qualified restoration providers

A controlled government procurement and project-matching environment — not an open exchange. Verified restoration needs are matched to qualified providers through transparent scoring, competitive procurement, and government approval, so restoration funding reaches the projects that retire the most environmental debt per dollar deployed.

Apply as a Restoration Provider View identified restoration needs

Qualified providers may include TerraReFlow and other approved operators. TerraReFlow is never automatically awarded public funds. All matching runs through transparent procurement, conflict-of-interest disclosure, open competition, and government approval.
